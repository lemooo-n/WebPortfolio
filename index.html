About Me

Hey there! I’m Jherico Luzano, a BSIT student with a passion for all things tech, design, and digital exploration. I specialize in web development, where I bring ideas to life through clean, responsive, and user-friendly websites. I’m always experimenting with new tools and frameworks, constantly looking to grow as both a developer and a creative.

Outside of the code editor, I’m a big believer in balance. I love to travel discovering new places, cultures, and stories that expand my perspective. And when I’m not out exploring the real world, you can probably find me in a digital one. I’m an avid gamer, and Genshin Impact is one of my all-time favorites. The game’s stunning world-building, design aesthetics, and attention to detail fuel my own creativity and inspire me to think beyond the ordinary in my work.

I’m also into storytelling, problem-solving, and turning small ideas into interactive experiences that people can connect with. Whether I’m building a website, writing a piece of code, or just leveling up in Teyvat, I’m always striving to create something meaningful.
